The one-sentence read. The moat was built first and at enormous volume — 218 backend PRs in the first five weeks — then the platform was hardened, then the frontend was migrated to React so it could be reskinned once, and only in the last fortnight has design led the queue. M1a is the first milestone where all four lanes move together.

Accounts, secrets, sandboxes

pre-milestone
47 PRs · first design PRs

Two RFCs (0009 auth, 0010 authorization) turned a single-user app into a multi-user one: identity persistence, OIDC, sessions, route access declarations, then a wholesale migration from Auth0 to Clerk. Lew's first two PRs land here — the sign-in sheet, and the colour-token pass that made Roadtrip themeable.

  • Backend front-loading was deliberate and it paid. 176 of 218 backend PRs landed before August. That is why M1a's asks are mostly small additions to finished systems rather than new subsystems.
  • Two closed PRs mark the milestone boundary. #639 (a park atlas) and #641 (trip templates) were opened 13 Aug and both closed unmerged the next day — real M1b work built ahead of the M1a surface it needed. The M1 document was written the day after, and exists because of them.
  • Volume is not velocity. The 117-PR week produced one data model; the 12-PR week produced four user-visible changes. The recent eras are smaller because the work is now shaped by a spec rather than by refactoring.
