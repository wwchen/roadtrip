03

What's next, and why in that order

M1a work, sequenced by what unblocks what. Dates are sequence, not commitment.

  • M1a-1 destination search & index (Will) — first, because #670 is already its frontend half and is sitting approved. Aligning the two is a conversation, not a build.
  • M1a-9 the date window (Lew) — the screen the whole surface hangs off, and the one two of three personas reach for first. The Crazy 8s sprint has already picked a direction, so this starts at drawing rather than at exploring.
  • M1a-6 site grid and M1a-7 nothing bookable (Lew) — the two states a popular campground actually shows. M1a-7 is the more common of the two and has never been designed.
  • Slice 4 — alerts in context, M1a-11 / 12 / 15 (Lew) — the backend is finished and shipping behind a form whose first field is a numeric POI ID. This is the cheapest user-visible win on the board. It also has a live bug in it: edit-mode save sends immutable fields and fails, so nobody is successfully editing alerts today.
  • M1a-10 freshness (Will) and M1a-17 filters (Lew) — both extend things that now exist rather than adding new systems.
Not on this board on purpose: M1a-13 (chances of getting in) was cut; M1b, M2 and M3 are named but unscheduled; and M1a-14 (Ask) waits on M2's verbs. Adding them here would make the next four weeks look busier and less true.
