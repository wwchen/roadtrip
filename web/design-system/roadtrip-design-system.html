Roadtrip Map \u2014 design language
\n

A quiet, data-dense system for planning trips on the map.

\n

Utilitarian and precise, in the spirit of Google Maps and Linear. The map is the canvas; every panel, drawer and control floats above it in the same dark, low-chroma surface family \u2014 so data reads first and chrome disappears.

\n
\n Route planning\n POI discovery\n Availability checking\n Availability monitoring\n Supercharger pricing\n
\n
\n\n \n
\n
\n 01\n

Color

\n
\n

Four roles, kept strictly apart. Neutrals build every surface. Blue is the only interactive color. Layer colors identify map data. Availability and status hues carry meaning, never decoration.

\n 03\n

Space, radius & elevation

\n
\n

A 4px grid. Controls sit at 6\u201310px radius; the mobile drawer opens at 18px; pills are fully round. Elevation is expressed only in shadow \u2014 surfaces stay flat and same-colored, so depth reads without borders fighting the map.
